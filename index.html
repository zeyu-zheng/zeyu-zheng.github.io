Hi! I am a third-year Ph.D. candidate in the ACO program at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prasad Tetali @ICARM and Sean Welleck @L3 Lab. I also worked with Boris Bukh before he joined OpenAI.

I train LLMs. I have worked on various aspects of foundation model development, including:

  • Data synthesis for mid- and post-training
  • Post-training algorithms and evaluation
  • Environments and infrastructure for agentic/coding RL

Previously, I received a B.S. Mathematics from Fudan University.

My Erdős number is 2.
